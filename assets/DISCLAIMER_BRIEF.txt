English Summary

This module only blocks preinstalled system components on a device you own and fully control from sending device identifiers, app usage records, or location data to vendor, carrier, or third-party servers. It does not break into, crack, or obtain data from anyone else's system.

Using it requires root and LSPosed. Rooting and system modification may void warranties, fail integrity checks, or cause system issues; you accept those risks. Blocking may degrade the affected components' features, and rules may lag behind or misjudge behavior on some OS versions.

The module is offline by default and collects or uploads nothing. It contacts api.github.com once only when you tap "Check for updates". All switches are stored locally.
